## 1. Operator and contact

Famie is operated by MAGIC METHODS, a sole proprietorship. Contact [support@famie.co](mailto:support@famie.co) for privacy inquiries and requests for access, correction or deletion. Legally required details of the Operator, including name and address, are also available on request without delay.

## 2. Information and purposes

Usernames, display names, email addresses if registered, password hashes, memberships, permissions and settings are used for authentication, group administration, preferences and account recovery. Real names and email addresses are optional, but content and images may identify people. Complete anonymity is not guaranteed.

Activity text, notes, images, likes and rewards are used for sharing, totals and history according to group permissions. Check images before uploading: they may contain information about people shown or image metadata.

Inquiries and records of operations, errors and access are used for responses, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, security, deletion and reapplication of deletion after restoration. Acceptance records are intended to contain the acting account's internal ID, document identity, version and content hash, acceptance or acknowledgment action, time and display language. Names, email addresses and content are not copied into these records, nor are IP addresses or browser details included. Acknowledgment of this Policy is not blanket consent to all processing.

## 3. Access and service providers

Content and member information are used according to group permissions. Viewers may save or forward content outside the group. The Operator handles information as necessary for service delivery, maintenance and inquiries.

External hosting and email service providers process information needed for server operation, automated email and inquiry correspondence. Current server-side storage, processing and operational work take place in Japan. The Operator performs operational work personally.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Disabling is not deletion. After confirmed closure or member deletion, identifying database fields are erased or replaced and activity text is cleared in batches. Images become inaccessible and physical deletion proceeds without an intentional grace period. Failed work is retried; immediate erasure of every copy is not guaranteed. Sent-like totals and records needed for consistency may remain.

Limited identity information is held encrypted for inquiry handling for 30 days (30 × 24 hours) from confirmation. It is not a recovery copy of text or images. Inquiries do not extend the period, and the information is not accessed after expiry. Acceptance records are intended to be retained during use and deleted when the relevant account is deleted.

Manageable copies of inquiry email are intended to be deleted 90 days after the case closes. Operational notifications are deleted when the response is complete after necessary records are prepared; notifications requiring no action are deleted after confirmation. Audit records follow a 90-day specification. The target maximum retention for Operator-managed web access, error and email delivery logs is normally 30 days; identification of the scope and implementation and verification of expiry management are currently in progress. Message content is distinct from delivery logs.

Ordinary backups may contain information predating deletion. The provider's whole-system backups follow a daily, maximum 15-generation specification and include encrypted inquiry files. Copies may remain until generations rotate after actual erasure. Disappearance of all copies a fixed number of days after the operation is not guaranteed. The 30-day access limit does not make remaining encrypted copies technically undecryptable.

Deletion and expiry are reapplied before service resumes after restoration. Records needed for reapplication are retained for 30 days after expiry of all backups capable of restoring deleted information and until necessary deletion work is complete. Specific records required for incident investigation or legal preservation may be retained to the extent necessary, with the need reviewed.

## 5. Security and requests

Measures include access controls, password hashing, encryption of limited identity information and keeping private keys off the server. Absolute security is not guaranteed. Requests are handled under applicable law with proportionate verification of identity and representative authority. Possession of a deletion confirmation code alone does not establish that the holder is the deleted person.

Application roles do not establish age, legal guardianship or family relationships. Minors must obtain consent from a legal representative where applicable law requires it.

## 6. Device storage and changes

See the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies/en/) for device storage. For material changes, the intended process is to display a summary and the document from the effective time and ask the family representative for renewed acknowledgment. Corrections without a change in meaning are announced in revision history. Required notices follow applicable law. The Japanese version is authoritative.
