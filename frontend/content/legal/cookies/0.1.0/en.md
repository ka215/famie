## 1. Cookies and device storage

Famie has processes using cookies, localStorage and PWA caches. The Operator is MAGIC METHODS, a sole proprietorship. Contact: [support@famie.co](mailto:support@famie.co).

| Name and mechanism | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| auth_token / cookie | Sign-in authentication | 30-day validity. Server-side tokens also expire |
| famie-theme / localStorage | Light/dark appearance | No automatic expiry. Saved on initial display based on system settings or defaults |
| famie.uiLocale.guest / localStorage | Language chosen while signed out | No automatic expiry. Saved when a language is selected |
| PWA cache | Delivery of static pages, scripts and similar resources | Depends on the browser and updates |

Signed-in language preferences are also stored with the account, separately from device storage. Cookies support authentication; theme and language support preferences; caches support delivery. Automatic initial theme storage is not treated as an act of consent.

## 2. Controls

Browser settings let you manage and delete cookies and site data. Restricting storage may prevent sign-in or preservation of preferences. Clearing device storage does not delete an account or its content. Also sign out on shared devices. Famie has no separate interface for granting or refusing storage permission.
